# Snowmoon: The Northern Corridor Breakthrough (Chapters 29–31)

An audiovisual adaptation of Chapters 29 through 31 of Vitalik Buterin's *Snowmoon*, produced for the POIDH open-source bounty under the **GNU General Public License v3.0**.

---

## 1. Project Overview & Narrative Arc

This project provides a book-accurate audiovisual reconstruction of the 14-hour battle of Elenar Forest and the liberation of Northglade:

* **Timeline:** Late Twilight on 3725 Snowmoon 29 through late afternoon on 3725 Snowmoon 30.
* **Core Canon Elements:**
  * Fiber-optic spooled snowmobile drones evading Arctic electronic jamming.
  * Mobile desktop printers deployed in the field to print physical adversarial countermeasures.
  * Real-time gradient descent simulation optimizing visual attack patterns against autonomous drone tanks.
  * The physical **"DOG"** adversarial patch disrupting the Arctic targeting sensor array.
  * The tragic loss of Den and Rear Base #3.
  * The final collapse from exhaustion and the public celebration in Northglade.

---

## 2. Character & Vehicle Design Assets

Reference model sheets used for visual consistency across all generation passes:

* **Tactician Zei:** Slender young male tactician with messy black hair, translucent HUD smart glasses, slate-grey sub-zero thermal parka, and high collar.
* **Veridian Field Infantry:** Rugged commanders and support soldiers in tactical arctic combat armor, insulated parkas, and frost-bitten face wraps.
* **Arctic Autonomous Drone Tanks:** Heavy, low-slung black matte combat units with angular faceted armor, active optical sensor turrets, and automated twin pulse cannons.

* **Scene:** Zei briefs field commanders atop a snowy ridge over a glowing cyan holographic map of Elenar Forest before the final countdown.
* **Prompt:**
  ```text

Frost-bitten wind whips through their parkas and pine trees in the background. The commanders nod with firm resolve before turning to depart as the tactician’s wrist device pulses with a soft amber warning chime. Crisp cold atmospheric lighting, subtle holographic glow.

Trailing spools of glowing fiber-optic cables unroll across fresh snow. Portable desktop printers strapped to cargo sleds hum with activity. High kinetic energy, low angle tracking shot, golden-orange dusk light filtering through frost-covered trees.

Segment 1.5: The Night Engagement
 * Timeline: Late Night (~22:00)
 * Scene: Veridian snow drones engaged in mobile night skirmishes across the frozen plain under starlight.
